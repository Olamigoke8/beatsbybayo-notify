Who I Am

Meet Bayo

For 25 years I worked as an X-ray tech — a job built on patience, calm under pressure, and paying close attention to the person in front of me. BeatsByBayo is that same care, poured into music. I'm not chasing flashy transitions or showing off behind the booth; I'm reading the room, playing songs all the way through, and making sure every guest — from the dance floor regulars to the ones hanging back — feels the joy of the night. Corporate, wedding, 50+ or private celebration: my goal is simple, bring people together and let the music do what it does best.
