Meet Bayo

A host who reads the room — not a playlist on shuffle.

For 25 years I worked as an X-ray tech — a job built on patience, calm under pressure, and paying close attention to the person in front of me. BeatsByBayo is that same care, poured into music.

I'm not chasing flashy transitions or showing off behind the booth. I'm reading the room, playing songs all the way through, and making sure every guest — from the dance floor regulars to the ones hanging back — feels the joy of the night.

  • Clean sound, clean presentation
  • Up-front about scope — no surprise fees
